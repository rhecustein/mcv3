## 💼 Brand Voice & Tone

### Writing Guidelines

**Do's:**
✅ Use clear, simple Indonesian
✅ Be professional yet approachable
✅ Focus on benefits, not just features
✅ Use active voice
✅ Include specific numbers/metrics
✅ Be empathetic to user pain points

**Don'ts:**
❌ Overly technical jargon (unless necessary)
❌ Exaggerated claims ("terbaik di dunia")
❌ Passive voice
❌ Overly casual/slang
❌ Negative framing

### Tone Examples

**Website Copy:**
```
❌ "SehatCert adalah platform terbaik untuk surat kesehatan"
✅ "SehatCert membantu 100+ klinik menghemat 5 jam/hari dalam penerbitan surat kesehatan"

❌ "Fitur canggih untuk manajemen outlet"
✅ "Kelola semua outlet Anda dari satu dashboard. Hemat waktu, tingkatkan efisiensi."

❌ "Sistem kami sangat secure dan reliable"
✅ "Data pasien Anda aman dengan enkripsi tingkat bank. Uptime 99.9% guaranteed."
```

**Customer Support:**
```
Tone: Helpful, patient, solution-oriented

Example:
❌ "Anda salah mengisi form"
✅ "Saya bantu isi formnya ya. Untuk kolom NIK, masukkan 16 digit..."

❌ "Fitur itu tidak bisa"
✅ "Saat ini fitur tersebut belum tersedia, tapi sudah dalam roadmap kami untuk Q3 2026"
```

- Net Promoter Score (NPS) (target: >50)

## 💡 Competitor Benchmarking

### Similar Platforms (Indonesia)

**Halodoc, Alodokter** (General telemedicine):
- Strength: Brand awareness, funding
- Weakness: Not specialized in health certificates
- Opportunity: SehatCert is niche-focused

**RyuGate** (Autobot's other product):
- Strength: Integration opportunity
- Weakness: Different target (attendance)
- Opportunity: Cross-sell potential

**Manual/Paper-based** (Current process):
- Weakness: Inefficient, prone to fraud
- Opportunity: Massive market to digitalize

### Differentiation Strategy

✅ **SehatCert Advantages:**
1. Specialized in health certificates only (laser focus)
2. Multi-outlet management built-in
3. Privacy-first company statistics
4. Custom template system (exclusive)
5. Affordable pricing for small clinics
6. Indonesian market-first approach
7. Excellent customer support (Indonesian language)
